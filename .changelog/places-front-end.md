### Added

- `studiometa/foehn-places` renders the place archive: `PlacesHooks` registers the `@places` Twig namespace (`templates/places/` of the child theme, then of the parent theme, then the package) and `@places-pkg` (the package only). The templates render the filter form, the facets, the address search, the summary, the list and map view switch, the list, the clustered map, the popup, the empty state and the pagination. A theme declares four sections, `place-filters`, `place-summary`, `place-results` and `place-pagination`, and passes `PlaceSearch::current()` as `result` and `PlacesView` as `places`. See the place locator guide
- The place templates emit declarative markup for `@studiometa/ui@next` and `@studiometa/ui-mapbox@next`: a filter change, an address result or Search this area updates the four sections with `Fetch` and keeps a normal history URL, and the map is never rebuilt. Install `@studiometa/js-toolkit@next`, `@studiometa/ui@next`, `@studiometa/ui-mapbox@next`, `mapbox-gl` and `@mapbox/mapbox-gl-geocoder`. Without JavaScript the archive is a searchable and filterable list
- The demo has a venue locator at `/venues/`, on `@studiometa/js-toolkit@next` and `@studiometa/ui@next`
